What I Believe

Empathy is a superpower

Understanding others is the hardest and most valuable skill in our industry. The best technical decisions come from deeply understanding the people they affect.

Kindness scales

The best engineers I've worked with were also the kindest. Across every team and company, this pattern holds. It's a competitive advantage hiding in plain sight.

The human side of engineering

Communication, mentoring, emotional intelligence - in engineering, these are what separate good individual contributors from great leaders. Technical depth alone won't get you there.

Great people make great products

Invest in building stronger teams and a stronger culture, and the products will follow. I've never seen a great product come from a team that wasn't thriving first.
